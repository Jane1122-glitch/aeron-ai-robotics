What is AERON?

AERON is an AI and robotics platform concept focused on bringing intelligent software and capable machines together in useful, human-centered ways.

What areas does AERON explore?

Our site introduces machine perception, adaptive intelligence, robotics, automation, and the workflows that connect them.

Can I try an AI feature on this website?

Yes. AI Studio can generate a social post, email draft, article outline, or short summary through the server-side Gemini integration. It does not control a robot.

Does the website connect to physical robots?

No. The site describes AI and robotics ideas, but it has no hardware connection or robot-control feature.

What happens to my AI Studio prompt?

The prompt is sent to the website backend and then to Google Gemini to generate a text draft. Avoid including sensitive or confidential details.
